FAQ

A few common
questions.

Clear answers are a good place to start. Specifics depend on your location, site and chosen equipment.

Ask us something else ↗
What happens when it isn’t sunny?+

Solar output changes with daylight and weather. A grid-connected setup can draw additional power when its solar generation is not enough; a battery may store some surplus for later, depending on the system.

What happens in a blackout?+

Many grid-connected systems switch off during a grid outage for safety. Backup power depends on the inverter, battery and system design—ask what a specific setup can support.

How does sending power back to the grid work?+

Some setups can export surplus electricity, but eligibility, metering and payment terms vary by location and energy retailer. Confirm the current terms for your property.

How long does a solar system last?+

Components have different lifespans and warranty terms. Compare the exact product documents and ask what is covered before you choose.

Should I choose panels only or add a battery?+

It depends on when you use energy, what storage would cost and the terms available where you live. Compare both options against your own needs rather than assuming a battery is always required.

Are there grants or subsidies available?+

Support programs vary by country and can change. Check current official information in your area before including a subsidy in your decision.
